First, get a free key.

The globe streams from Cesium, and a key is how it knows the requests are yours. It is free and takes about a minute.

On Cesium the key is called an access token — copy the one named Default. If it offers to add “Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles”, say yes.

Paste your key.

It is kept in this browser and sent only to the map service.

Settings

Light

The moment the daylight solve landed on. Pick a light preset and the app searches the year for a time when the sun over this coordinate sits at that elevation.

Atmosphere

Clouds cast on the ground below. Costs a shadow pass, so it only runs in daylight and close in.

Recording

Frames render one at a time on a fixed clock, so the export is smooth even when the flight is not. It takes longer than the clip.
